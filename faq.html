Frequently Asked Questions

▶ How do I place an order for delivery?

You can place an order by calling us at +250 798 123 456 or using our online ordering system. We deliver within a 10-mile radius. Minimum order is $15.

▶ Do you offer vegetarian and vegan options?

Yes! Many of our dishes are vegetarian-friendly, and we have dedicated vegan options as well. Just let us know your dietary preferences when ordering.

▶ How far in advance should I book catering?

We recommend booking at least 2 weeks in advance for events with 50+ guests. For smaller events, 1 week notice is usually sufficient. Contact us for last-minute availability.

▶ Do you offer cooking classes for beginners?

Absolutely! Our beginner classes cover basic knife skills, cooking techniques, and simple recipes. No experience necessary. Check our services page for schedules.

▶ What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), and mobile payments like PayPal.
